What I Learned

Three Honest Takeaways

1️⃣

AI amplifies your prompt quality

"Write tests" generated 4,000 lines of tech debt. A specific prompt with modular structure would have saved Phase 2 entirely.

2️⃣

Refactoring finds bugs that testing misses

A race condition lived for 2 months. 1,000+ tests ran daily. None caught it. Reading the code found it in 5 minutes.

3️⃣

Free AI is a treadmill, not a foundation

17 tools in 2 months. 7 are gone. 5 remain. Diversify, rotate, never depend on a single provider.

"The biggest lesson? Sometimes the best test improvement is deleting 4,000 lines."
